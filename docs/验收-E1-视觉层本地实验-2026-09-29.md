## 四、三种模式拼接结果（General，题 1「雨天窗边」为样例）

- **legacy**：完全旧路径，prompt 与改动前逐字节一致（旧 smoke 全绿背书）。
- **sleepy-default** positive：
  `masterpiece, best quality, highly detailed, empty of people, no human subject, the environment or object is the clear main subject, 雨天窗边, natural realistic photography, believable spatial relationships, …, visually pleasing but unposed`
  negative：`lowres, worst quality, low quality, jpeg artifacts, blurry, people, person, human, face, portrait, selfie, random passerby, unwanted character, text, watermark, logo, oversaturated colors, plastic texture, staged product shot`
- **custom**（例词 `flat graphic illustration, limited color palette`）positive 同层序但视觉层换成自定义词；negative **不含** oversaturated/plastic/staged（审美负面仅 sleepy-default），结构负面保留。
- NAI 同构：质量头 `best quality, amazing quality, very aesthetic, absurdres`，视觉底座 slice-of-life 版，结构负面含 `1girl/1boy`，审美负面 `overly glossy, plastic texture, promotional poster`。
